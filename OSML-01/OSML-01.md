OPTICAL INTELLIGENCE STORAGE MODEL LAB

OSML-01
Optical AI Model Storage and Retrieval

Mission

Develop a new neural-network computing architecture that operates with model weights residing primarily on optical media, retrieving and processing parameters on demand without requiring the complete model to reside in RAM.

Long-term vision

Investigate whether optical media can become a practical component of a new AI memory and computing architecture, potentially integrating optical storage, electronic processing, and silicon photonics.

Innovation 1 - Optical AI Memory

Using optical media as a high-capacity, long-term storage medium for neural-network weights.

Innovation 2 - Optical AI Computing

Using light to perform neural-network calculations, potentially reducing dependence on conventional electronic computation.
