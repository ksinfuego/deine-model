SILICON INTELLIGENCE MODEL LAB

SIML-01
Analog Intelligence and Neuromorphic Computing Research

Mission

Research and develop alternative artificial intelligence architectures using analog electronics, semiconductor devices, neuromorphic computation, and physical information processing.

Long-term vision

Create an independently designed silicon-based AI system, from mathematical model to physical semiconductor implementation.

Research Questions

Can we develop an analog-native artificial intelligence system in which computation, memory, and learning are implemented through the physical behavior of programmable semiconductor circuits, rather than relying primarily on conventional digital processing?

Innovation 1 — Better way to implement existing AI

Take an existing neural network and develop a better physical implementation using analog silicon.

Innovation 2 — Better way to create AI itself

Develop an AI model whose mathematical structure and learning mechanisms are designed specifically around the physical characteristics of silicon.
